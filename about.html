Mandi Den began with the aroma of rice bubbling in a pot, stories traded over dinner and the feeling that there should always be one more place at the table.

We bring the generous spirit of Arabian mandi together with Indian flavors we grew up loving. The result is a welcoming neighborhood restaurant where food is meant to be passed around.

From the first marinade to the last cup of chai, we make each visit feel like a meal with people who know your name.
